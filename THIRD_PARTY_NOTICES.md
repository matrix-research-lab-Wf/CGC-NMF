# Third-party notices

No third-party dataset other than the attributed CC BY 4.0 Optdigits derivative
is intentionally redistributed.

The former Niclas Borlin `hungarian.m` file was removed because a clear
redistribution license could not be established. It was replaced with the
original base-MATLAB implementation `code/core/assignment_min_cost.m`, which was
validated against the historical routine on 1,200 seeded random square cost
matrices of orders 1 through 12.

The ERDNMF and SNMFWLP wrappers implement published multiplicative update
equations and the experiment protocol used in this study. They are not copies of
complete third-party repositories. Their source comments and the manuscript
identify the relevant methods. If any file is later replaced by upstream code,
its upstream license and copyright notice must be added before redistribution.
