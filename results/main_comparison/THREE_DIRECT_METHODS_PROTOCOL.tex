\subsection{Unified comparison of direct clustering methods}
\label{subsec:unified_direct_comparison}

We conducted a unified comparison among GNMFLD, GOCNMF, and
CGC-GOCNMF. All three methods obtain clustering assignments directly from the
largest coordinate in each row of the learned representation matrix; no
\(K\)-means algorithm or external classifier was used. For every dataset and
trial, the methods used the same data normalization, labeled subset, random
seed, initial nonnegative factors, and evaluation code. Ten percent of the
samples in each class were selected as labeled data. The primary ACC and NMI
values were computed only on the remaining unlabeled samples.

GNMFLD was implemented as
\[
\min_{U,V\geq0}
 \|X-UV^{T}\|_{F}^{2}
 +\alpha\|P_{\Omega}(V)-P_{\Omega}(Y)\|_{F}^{2}
 +\beta\operatorname{Tr}(V^{T}LV),
\]
with a binary five-nearest-neighbor graph and 200 multiplicative-update
iterations. GOCNMF and CGC-GOCNMF used the previously fixed dataset-specific
values of \(p\) and the graph coefficient, with 50 iterations. CGC-GOCNMF
differed from GOCNMF only through the out-of-fold conservative calibration of
the graph.

The GNMFLD parameters for PIE, YaleB, COIL20, and MNIST were taken from
published follow-up reproductions of this baseline. Directly verifiable
dataset-specific values were not located for COIL100 and Optdigits; therefore,
the corresponding values were transferred from COIL20 and MNIST,
respectively, and are explicitly marked as transferred rather than
original-paper settings. All results were averaged over 20 paired trials.
