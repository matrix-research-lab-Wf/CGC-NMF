\subsection{Unified end-to-end computational cost}
\label{subsec:end_to_end_time}

We conducted a dedicated timing experiment because the training times recorded
during the accuracy comparison did not include all method-specific
preprocessing. For each method, the reported end-to-end cost was defined as
\[
T_{\mathrm{total}}
=
T_{\mathrm{graph}}
+
T_{\mathrm{calibration}}
+
T_{\mathrm{factorization}}.
\]
Here, \(T_{\mathrm{calibration}}=0\) for GNMFLD and GOCNMF, whereas for
CGC-GOCNMF it contains the complete five-fold out-of-fold Brier evaluation
and computation of \(\theta\). Data loading, common column normalization, and
post-training evaluation were excluded.

Each method independently reconstructed its graph from the normalized data
inside the timed block. No graph, calibration result, or factorization output
was shared between methods. In particular, CGC-GOCNMF performed an independent
factorization even when \(\theta=0\). The three methods used the same labeled
subset and initial factors in each paired trial, while their execution order
was randomized reproducibly to reduce fixed-order and cache effects. Before
measurement, every method was run once as an untimed warm-up on each dataset.
The final results are reported as the mean and standard deviation over 20
paired trials on the same hardware and MATLAB installation.
